# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: Sarawakians living abroad (diaspora) — emotional connection to home, want to express love for Sarawak.
Secondary: Government agencies and potential partners evaluating the campaign for official endorsement or collaboration.
Tertiary: General public and media discovering the campaign.

## Product Purpose

A national heritage campaign that collects love messages from Sarawakians worldwide and plants them as hearts on a Sarawak division map. Success = government agencies see it as credible enough to co-brand or partner with. Every submission is a data point that proves the campaign's reach and emotional resonance.

## Brand Personality

Proud. Official. Warm.

Voice: Dignified but human. The authority of a state tourism board with the warmth of a community movement. Not a fan site. Not a charity. A campaign backed by data and love.

Emotional goal: A government officer opening the site should feel "this is something we can put our logo on."

## Anti-references

- Fan sites and personal blogs — busy, unofficial, unpolished
- Social media campaigns — casual, ephemeral, trend-chasing
- Generic charity websites — dated, low-budget, clip-art feel
- SaaS startup landing pages — metric cards, tech gradients, cold aesthetic
- The current version — too much red, pua kumbu as wallpaper, "serabut" (cluttered)

## Design Principles

1. **Authority before emotion** — establish credibility first, then let the warmth come through. Government partners need to trust before they feel.
2. **Sarawak as accent, not wallpaper** — cultural elements (hornbill, pua kumbu) appear as deliberate accents, never as repeated background patterns.
3. **Data tells the story** — real submission counts, division map hearts, and country reach are the proof points. Make numbers feel significant.
4. **One thing at a time** — each section has one job. No section competes with another.
5. **Space is respect** — generous white space signals confidence and professionalism. Cramped layouts signal amateur.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast)
- 4-language support already implemented (EN/MS/Iban/ZH) — preserve
- Reduced motion support for all animations
- Mobile-first responsive
